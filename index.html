How it works

The format
Every round is scored in Stableford points. Your best 6 rounds count — submit up to 12.
The total
Your six highest scores are added together. Highest combined total leads the order of merit.
Ties
Split on countback — best single round, then second-best, and so on. Genuine ties share the spot.
The season
Runs to —. Gold, silver and bronze are decided when the last card lands.
